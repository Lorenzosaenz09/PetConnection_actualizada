ABOUT PETCONNECTION

Every great generation
starts with the right match.

PetConnection is a platform designed to help dog owners find potential breeding partners for their pets. It brings the familiar simplicity of a dating app to the process of discovering possible matches for responsible breeding.

Create your dog's profile, discover other dogs, explore potential matches and connect with their owners.
